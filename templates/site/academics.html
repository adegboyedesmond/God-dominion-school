Senior secondary (SS 1–3)

In SS 1 every student chooses a stream with guidance from teachers and parents. All streams take English, Mathematics, Civic Education and a trade subject.

Science

Commercial

Arts & humanities

For future lawyers, writers and leaders

Literature in English, Government, CRS, History, Fine Arts, a Nigerian language.

SS 3 students write WAEC and NECO, with after-school JAMB and post-UTME classes from SS 2.
