Admission questions

Which classes can I apply into?

JSS 1 to SS 2. We do not admit new students into SS 3 because of WAEC registration.

Is there an application fee?

Applying online is free. The entrance examination form fee is paid at the school on exam day.

How much are school fees?

Fees depend on the class. Call the office or visit us for the current fee schedule. Once admitted, fees can be paid online through the student portal.

Do you accept students of other faiths?

Yes. We are a Christian school and all students attend devotion, but we welcome every family that respects our values.

Can we visit before applying?

Yes. Visits are welcome on weekdays. Book a visit so a staff member can show you around.
