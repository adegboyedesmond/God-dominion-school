How to join God Dominion School

The whole process takes about two weeks and starts online.

  1. Apply onlineFill the admission form. You get an application number straight away.
  2. Entrance examWe invite your child for a short test in English and Mathematics.
  3. Meet the schoolA brief chat with parents and the child, and a tour of the campus.
  4. Receive admissionPay the acceptance fee and your child gets a portal login.
